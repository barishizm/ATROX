# Surface brief: web/index.html (home)

Scope: ATROX home page, mode Persuade. Visitor: a lifter who wants to know how strong they are relative to people like them. Job: understand the Strength Score in one look, trust how data is handled, join the waiting list (mailto hello@atrox.online until a form exists). Constraints: static HTML/CSS, TR+EN, no invented claims, app screens real (simulator, seeded demo data labelled sample), legal pages and /u/ invite page keep working.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one powerlifting meet scoresheet seen once, not a landing page that quotes one. It refuses the hero-plus-feature-cards startup template: the offer is a filled-in attempt card whose last column is the percentile.
OWN-WORLD: Brand ground #21232E (night gym), ruled hairlines in a lighter tint, one condensed grotesque used at wide ranges (condensed caps for form labels, tabular numerals for kg and percentile), three round referee lights (white lights lit = a lift counted; blue #105BFF and orange #FE8500 are the only other inks). No cards, no icons, no gradients, no shadows. Recognisable with content removed: a ruled grid of boxed fields and three lamp circles.
STORY: A visitor sees a filled sheet for a sample lifter, reads that each lift ends in a percentile against comparable lifters, understands the three-line method (class, model, on-device), sees the real app, reads what the AI Coach does and does not receive, then signs up by email.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sheet header (ATROX, sample lifter class: M, 25-29, 80 kg, labelled SAMPLE). Title "Strength Score" in huge condensed caps. Three ruled rows (squat, bench, deadlift) with kg, percentile, and three lamps each that light white in sequence once on load. The total score row sits at the fold with the primary action (join the waiting list) as the sheet's signature box. Language switch EN/TR top right.
